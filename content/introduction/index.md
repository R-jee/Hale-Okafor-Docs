## The theme in brief

- **Every product is an object record.** An object number, measurements drawn as dimension lines, materials, the year and the maker, an edition state ("1 of 1", "Edition of 12, 4 remain", "Made to order, ships in 6 weeks"), a specification ledger and a dated making log.
- **Sold pieces stay on record.** They're stamped Sold, move to an archive, and offer "Commission something similar" instead of disappearing.
- **Size you can trust.** Scale drawings beside a coin, a hand, an A4 sheet, a chair or a person, and a "See it at true size" view calibrated to the visitor's screen.
- **Ways to buy that suit handmade work.** Sealed-bid lots in a sale room, holds on one-of-a-kind pieces, offers, commissions taken as work orders, and studio visits.
- **Built for a real shop.** Ten languages including right-to-left Arabic, Hebrew and Urdu, local currencies, filters, quick view, a packing-list cart, predictive search, accessibility to WCAG 2.2 AA and no app required for any of it.

> Note: The demo store is **Hale & Okafor**, a two-person ceramics studio in Leeds. Every screenshot in these docs is taken from it, so you can open the same page and compare.
