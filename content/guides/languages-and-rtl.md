## The ten languages

| Language | Code | Direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| English | `en` | Left to right |
| French | `fr` | Left to right |
| German | `de` | Left to right |
| Spanish | `es` | Left to right |
| Japanese | `ja` | Left to right |
| Korean | `ko` | Left to right |
| Simplified Chinese | `zh-CN` | Left to right |
| Hebrew | `he` | Right to left |
| Arabic | `ar` | Right to left |
| Urdu | `ur` | Right to left |

Every word the theme itself prints (buttons, labels, filters, sort names, dates, the cart, forms, errors, accessibility labels) is translated in its language files.

## Turning a language on

1. In the Shopify admin, go to **Settings → Languages → Add language**, choose it and **Publish**.
2. Translate your own content (products, collections, pages, menus, metafields, metaobjects) with Shopify's free **Translate & Adapt** app, or import translations.
3. The language and country panel appears in the header and footer automatically once a store has more than one language or market.

## What the theme translates for you

- **Filter values and tags.** Shopify can't translate tags, so the theme carries translations for common values (glazes, clays, forms, finishes) and shows them in the visitor's language.
- **Sort names**, which Shopify gives only in the store's main language.
- **Dates and months** in the sale room, release record and journal.
- **Glaze tiles and swatches**: options are recognised by their translated names, so color tiles still show in every language.
- **Film descriptions**, from the section heading.

## Right to left

In Arabic, Hebrew and Urdu the whole layout mirrors: the header, drawers (the cart opens from the left), carousels and swipes, dimension lines, the shelf, seals and ring text, the hero's halves, chevrons and arrows. Number ranges (`12–60 cm`, prices) keep reading left to right inside right-to-left text.

## Chinese, Japanese and Korean

Han, kana and hangul are set solid (without the Latin letter spacing used for labels), and page titles are translated.
