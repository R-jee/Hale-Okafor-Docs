## The packing list

- Each line shows the object's photo, number, measurements, weight and chosen options, with quantity and remove.
- A **packing note** sums the pieces and the parcel weight and flags fragile handling (`atelier.fragile`).
- **Estimated delivery** dates come from **Theme settings → [Shipping and delivery](../theme-settings/shipping-and-delivery.html)**, the same dates as on the product page.
- **Order note** and **Discount code** fold away until needed. An invalid discount code is explained, not silently ignored.
- **Often paired with** suggests one or a few objects you choose (**Theme settings → Cart**).
- An optional **Gift wrap** product can be added with one tick.
- **Express checkout** buttons (Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal) can show under the total.
- The empty cart shows an empty crate and objects to start with.

Choose **Drawer** or **Page** in **Theme settings → [Cart](../theme-settings/cart.html) → Cart type**. The cart page works without JavaScript.

## Checkout and customer accounts

Shopify hosts checkout and the new customer accounts (sign in, sign up, orders, profile). Atelier's demo brands them with the theme's colors, fonts, corner radius and logo through Shopify's checkout branding. To match your own store, use **Settings → Checkout → Customize** and **Settings → Customer accounts**.

The account icon in the header uses Shopify's account component: sign in, or the customer's name and orders once signed in.
