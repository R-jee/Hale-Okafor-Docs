## Blog

- The journal opens with the [Film strip](../sections/film-strip.html) in the demo, then the articles.
- Notes show as index cards under a measured heading. Tags show as index tabs (plain links, so they're shareable and work without JavaScript); the current tag is marked, and tags are translated where the theme knows them.
- Choose articles per page, columns, and whether cards show the image, date, author and excerpt.

## Article

- A note is set like an essay in a catalogue: a record line (journal, date, author), the title, its tags as materials, the image as a numbered plate, and the text with numbered section headings.
- After the text: the objects of the series the note is about (the first tag that names a collection), a share button, and links to newer and older notes.
- Each part can be switched off in the section: featured image (and its shape), the link to the blog, date, author, tags, the series, share and the newer/older links. **Tags not to show** keeps internal tags hidden.
