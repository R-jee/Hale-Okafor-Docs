## Setting it up

1. Open **Theme settings → [Logo and favicon](../theme-settings/logo-and-favicon.html)**.
2. Under **Logos by language**, fill in **Languages** with the language codes that should use another logo, separated by commas (for example `ar, ur`).
3. Upload the **Logo** for those languages, and a **Light logo** for dark grounds.
4. There are two slots, so you can set one logo for one group of languages and another for a second group (for example `ar, ur` and `ja, zh-CN, ko`).

Languages not listed keep the main logo. The logo's alt text is your store name.
