## The country and language panel

The header shows the current market as `US · USD · EN` with a flag. It opens a compact panel with the countries and the languages. The same panel sits in the footer. It appears when your store has more than one market or language.

## Setting up markets

1. In **Settings → Markets**, add your markets and turn on **local currencies** for them.
2. Make sure your **shipping profile** reaches those countries (**Settings → Shipping and delivery**). Shopify only sells a product in a country your shipping reaches; without it, products show as sold out abroad.
3. Prices convert automatically, or set fixed prices per market.

> Tip: A "Rest of world" shipping zone is the simplest way to make every market purchasable while you set up rates.

## Currency symbols

Prices show the symbol of the shopper's currency (`$`, `€`, `£`, `¥`, `₩`). The cart total also shows the currency code (`$145.00 USD`), so shoppers know which dollar they're paying in. Set the format in **Settings → General → Store currency → Change formatting**.
