## Adding a film

1. In the section, choose the **Video** (upload it there or pick one from **Content → Files**). MP4 works best, ideally under 20 MB and about 1080 px wide.
2. Shopify makes a preview image from the video; it shows while the film loads and when motion is reduced. To change it, edit the video in **Content → Files**.
3. Write the **Small label**, **Heading** and **Text**, and an optional **Button**. The heading is also the film's description for screen readers, in the visitor's language (Shopify can't translate a video file's own alt text).

## Behaviour

- Films start muted when they scroll into view and pause when they leave it, so only one plays at a time.
- A sound button sits on each film, with its label translated.
- With **reduced motion** (the visitor's system setting, or **Theme settings → Animations** turned down) films don't start by themselves and decorative movement stays still.
- Chapters (Film strip) and steps (Film wheel) are blocks you add in the section.
