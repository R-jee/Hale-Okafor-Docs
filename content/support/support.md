## Before you contact us

1. Look for your question in the [FAQ](faq.html) and in the [Guides](../guides/the-object-record.html).
2. Check the section or block's page in these docs: every setting is listed with what it does.
3. Compare with the [demo store](https://hale-okafor-zjf5mtr1.myshopify.com), which uses the same settings as a fresh install.

## Contact

<p><a class="button button--fire" href="https://forms.gle/GX1VWC5YcmNa83U46" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open the support form</a></p>

Send your question through the [support form](https://forms.gle/GX1VWC5YcmNa83U46) (a Google Form). It's also linked from the theme's page in the Shopify Theme Store. Include:

- your store's address (`your-store.myshopify.com`),
- the page and section or block you're working on,
- a screenshot or short screen recording,
- what you expected and what happened.


## What support covers

| Covered | Not covered |
| --- | --- |
| Questions about the theme's features and settings | Custom code changes or new features |
| Bugs in the theme's own code | Apps, and code added by apps or other developers |
| Help setting up metafields and templates the theme uses | Store setup unrelated to the theme (payments, taxes, shipping rates) |

For custom work, hire a developer from the [Shopify Partner directory](https://www.shopify.com/partners/directory).

## Updating the theme

Theme updates replace the theme's code. Your settings and content carry over; **code edits don't**. Before editing code, duplicate the theme (**Online Store → Themes → ⋯ → Duplicate**) and keep a note of your changes.

## Policies

- **Accessibility:** the theme is built to WCAG 2.2 AA. Please report any barrier you find through support.
- **Privacy:** the theme loads no tracking, fonts or scripts from third parties beyond Shopify's own.
