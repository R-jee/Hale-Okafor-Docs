Changelog

What changed in each version of the theme.

All notable changes to the Hale Okafor theme are recorded here. The format follows Keep a Changelog.

#[1.0.0] - 2026-10-24

The first release of Hale Okafor, a theme for makers who sell one-of-a-kind and small-batch objects. Documentation: https://r-jee.github.io/Hale-Okafor-Docs/

#The object record

  • Every product as a catalogued object: object number, name and wall label (material · year · maker), edition state ("1 of 1", "Edition of 12, 4 remain", "Made to order, ships in 6 weeks"), a specification ledger and a dated making log, from native metafields and metaobjects. Each part appears only when its data exists.
  • Plates with dimension lines drawn from the object's measurements, plate numbers and a contact sheet; zoom, video and 3D models.
  • Size three ways: dimension lines, a scale drawing beside a coin, a hand, an A4 sheet, a chair or a person, and a true-size view calibrated to the visitor's screen.
  • The Studio variant picker: glazes as glazed test tiles, sizes drawn to scale, other options as a ledger; buttons, swatches and dropdowns too, supporting up to 2048 variants and combined listings.
  • Reserve a piece, make an offer, a certificate of authenticity, the maker card, stock signal, delivery estimate, complementary products, share, rating and volume pricing blocks.
  • Sold pieces stay on record: a Sold stamp, the archive, and "Commission something similar".
  • Alternate templates for sale room lots, editions and made-to-order pieces.

#Collections, search and cart

  • The Shelf (objects at their relative real heights on a shared baseline) and the Index (a catalogue table), switched by the shopper.
  • Filters as a ledger, catalogue index tags (kind, material, technique, maker, year) that work without apps or JavaScript, removable chips, a helpful empty state and sorting with translated names.
  • Predictive search as a card catalogue, with object numbers, series, journal and pages.
  • The cart as a packing list: object records, parcel weight, fragile handling, estimated delivery, order note, discount code, one paired object, gift wrap and express checkout; as a drawer or a page.
  • Quick view and quick add from every card.

#Sections

  • Home and story: Kiln stage, Catalogue contents, Catalogue spreads, Kiln firing, Shelf, Object record, Wall text, Making log, Materials index, Glaze matcher, Plate, Studio plate, Series sheet, Archive, Maker, Studio placard, Journal notes and Questions.
  • Five film sections, each framing a video differently: Film arch, Film strip, Film vitrine, Film wheel and Film window.
  • Sale room (sealed-bid lots), Release record, Commission form (work order or contact), Visit booking and Exhibition CV.
  • A label-strip header with object counts and a Bids open tag; a colophon footer with Explore, Legal and About columns.
  • Journal, article, 404, password and gift card pages designed for the theme.

#International and accessibility

  • Ten languages: English, French, German, Spanish, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, Hebrew, Arabic and Urdu, with right-to-left layouts throughout.
  • A compact language and country panel with local currencies; a logo per language for the header and footer.
  • Built to WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard support, focus handling, announcements, contrast-checked color schemes, 24 px touch targets and reduced motion respected.

#Tooling

  • No framework and no build step; components load only when needed, under a size budget.
  • Linting, type checks, unit tests, Playwright end-to-end tests with axe, Lighthouse CI and Theme Check.
  • A seed script that builds the complete demo store from one catalog file.
