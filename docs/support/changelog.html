Changelog

What changed in each version of the theme.

All notable changes to Atelier are recorded here. The format follows Keep a Changelog.

#[Unreleased]

#Added

  • Atelier foundations: the Studio settings (object numbers, units, dimension lines, scale reference, shelf view and size range, sold objects, edition label), a record font, four color schemes and the Kiln style.
  • The object record: atelier.* metafields and the material, technique, maker, series, making step and exhibition metaobjects, with snippets that degrade quietly without them.
  • Product page: plates with dimension lines and a contact sheet; object header, edition and status (with an edition ledger), specification, scale drawing, making log and maker blocks; edition and commission templates; sold objects lead to a commission.
  • Collection and search: series sheet, the Shelf (objects at relative height) and the Index, switched by the shopper; sold objects stamped, archived or hidden; filters as a ledger; related objects from the same series.
  • Cart: a packing list with each object's record, a packing note (pieces, parcel weight, handling, dispatch date for made-to-order pieces), one paired object and an empty crate.
  • Predictive search as a card catalogue with tab dividers, and series to browse when nothing matches.
  • Shelf section; Kiln demo catalog (21 ceramics), demo pages and logo.
  • Sections: Studio plate, Object record, Making log, Materials index, Series sheet, Archive, Commission form (work order or contact), Release record, Exhibition CV, Wall text, Plate, Maker, Studio placard, Journal notes and Questions; contact, commission and studio page templates; the Kiln home and studio pages.
  • Header as a label strip with object counts and a measured rule; footer as a colophon written from the theme settings.
  • Journal as index cards with tag tabs; articles set as catalogue essays (record line, numbered plate and section headings, the series a note is about); 404 as an object not in the catalogue with a shelf of ones that are; password page as a closed kiln; gift card as a catalogue card.

#Changed

  • Atelier replaces the Solenne design layer (ADR-007). Solenne's sections, styles and signature effects remain on the main branch; the engine and tooling carry over.

#Removed

  • The stock contact form, the capsule header, mega menu promo cards and products, the thirteen footer layouts, shaped back-to-top buttons, the footer reveal and clock, and the logo, image, collections, app badge and promise footer blocks.
  • Generic and trend-led sections (slideshow, image with text, multicolumn, rich text, featured collection, bento grid, tickers, counters, stacked cards, parallax and orbit sections and others), the Signature settings, the Meridian, Noctis, Terra and Ember styles and their demo stores.

#Solenne history

#Added

  • Theme foundation on the Elevra engine: core modules, cart, filters, product form and gallery, predictive search, seven storefront languages, tooling and CI.
  • Signature settings: image shape (rectangle, arch, soft), product card style (minimal, lifted, framed), button hover (fill, sweep, glow), glow color, paper grain texture, reading progress line.
  • Signature sections: Solar hero, Horizon ticker, Arch collections, Product rail, Manifesto, Stacked cards, Ritual steps, Bento grid, Orbit features, Parallax columns, Counter stats.
  • Product page: Highlights and Notes blocks, shaped gallery images, glowing information panel.
  • Collection page: Arch banner with product count and quick-link pills; Solenne checkboxes, pill filter bar and chips; second card image wipes in on hover.
  • Header shape: floating capsule. Footer layout: Horizon.
  • Five styles: Solenne, Meridian, Noctis, Terra and Ember, each with its own fonts, schemes, shapes and demo pages.
  • Eclipse slider: full-bleed hero slides revealed through a circle, curtain or arch, with progress tabs and a pause button.
  • Header: third-level links open as flyouts beside dropdowns; "Always open as a dropdown" setting.
  • Collection: two-handle price slider, 2 to 5 products per row, promo tile in the product grid.
  • Templates: product boxed, grid and editorial; collection drawer, sidebar and categories.
  • Signature hero: five art-directed home page openers, one per style: Halo (arched photo in candlelight), Summit (full-bleed photo cut by a ridge line, coordinates readout), Dial (photo in a watch dial with a spec sheet), Bloom (three photos in organic shapes) and Pour (round photo in a text ring, stickers and a band of words). Scroll-driven motion.
  • Footer layouts: Lantern, Atlas, Chronograph (the visitor's local time on a dial), Canopy and Crema, one per style.
  • Back to top: shaped buttons whose outline fills as the page scrolls: candle, rounded box, watch, clover and kettle (Footer → Back to top style), one per style.
  • Testimonials: Spotlight and Marquee layouts and an optional average-rating summary.
  • Countdown: beside-an-image layout, tile or large digits, image badge and a discount code with a Copy button.
  • Promo popup: a large offer ("15% off your first order"), Ticket style with a coupon stub, and a discount code with a Copy button.
  • Filters: pills style, swatch grid, image values, switches for yes/no filters, "Show more" past a set number of values, search inside long filters, quick price ranges and a choice of which filters start open.
  • Demo logos: each demo store has its own logo (mark and wordmark in the style's font), a light version for the transparent header and a favicon, drawn by scripts/seed/branding/make-logos.js and set by the seed's new branding step.
  • Demo stores: each style opens with its own Signature hero and footer, adds a sale countdown, testimonials and a welcome popup, and the seed creates the codes they show (new discounts step, needs the write_discounts scope).
  • Demo catalogs: home fragrance, travel and outdoor, watches and jewelry, plants and ceramics, coffee and tea: 130 products and 51 collections, generated from compact definitions.

#Changed

  • The five demo home pages open with the Signature hero instead of the Eclipse slider (still available as a section).
  • Search, localization and cart drawer styles load without blocking the first paint.

#Fixed

  • A strip of page background showed between the announcement bar and the capsule header over a full-bleed hero.
  • The footer wordmark ran past the edge with wide fonts (Meridian's Unbounded), so the page scrolled sideways; it now fits the footer.
  • Bento grid tiles and the Orbit features ring could widen the page on phones.
  • The promo popup opened in the corner instead of the middle of the screen.

#Removed

  • Elevra's Editorial hero, Story, Lookbook carousel, Collection showcase and Heading with images sections.
