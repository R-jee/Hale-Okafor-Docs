Sections overview

Hale & Okafor has 35 sections you can add from the theme editor, plus the main sections of each template. None of them are Shopify's default sections: each was designed for the theme.

#Home and story sections

The studio catalogue: openings, shelves, records, wall text and the rest of the sections a merchant arranges on the home page and content pages.

ArchiveSold pieces kept on record, as a wall of numbered tags.Catalogue contentsThe store's series set like the contents page of an exhibition catalogue, at poster scale.Catalogue spreadsThe series as the double-page spreads of a printed catalogue, passing sideways as the page scrolls down (held on screen meanwhile, large…Glaze matcherBuild a set that belongs together.Journal notesThe latest studio notes from a blog, as index cards.Kiln firingThe studio's firing as a held scene on a dark ground.Kiln stageThe home page's opening, a lit vitrine held on screen while the page scrolls past it.MakerA maker's profile from their maker metaobject (portrait, name, where they work, biography) beside a short shelf of their objects, from a…Making logHow the studio works, as a dated log sheet.Materials indexThe studio's materials as numbered glaze test tiles (M-01, M-02 …): each tile is glazed in the material's color (its color field, or the…Object recordOne product's full record anywhere in the store.PlateA photograph set like a numbered figure in a catalogue ("Pl.QuestionsCare, shipping and commission questions as a numbered ledger (Q.01, Q.02 …).Series sheetIntroduces one series (a collection) as a catalogue sheet.ShelfA collection (usually a series) as objects standing side by side on one baseline, each drawn at its relative height, under a measured…Studio placardVisiting the studio, set like the engraved placard by a workshop door.Studio plateThe opening of the home page.Wall textA statement set like the text panel at the entrance of an exhibition.

#Film sections

Five video sections, each with its own way of framing a film: an arch, a strip, a vitrine, a wheel and a window.

Arch cinemaThe studio's film shown through a kiln arch on a dark ground.Film stripThe studio's film run through a strip of 35mm stock, tilted a little on the page.VitrineThe studio's film shown as a museum piece, inside a glass case on a plinth under a spotlight.WheelThe studio's film seen from above, as a round disc on a banding wheel.Studio windowThe studio's film seen through a tall mullioned window.

#Sale room and commissions

Sections that sell differently: sealed-bid lots, release records, commissions and studio visits.

Commission formA work order sent to the studio (Shopify's contact form, so it works without JavaScript.Exhibition CVExhibitions, stockists and press listed year by year, the way a maker's CV is set.Release recordThe next kiln opening or release.Sale roomAntique lots offered by sealed bid, in layers on a dark ground.Visit bookingAsk for a visit to the studio.
