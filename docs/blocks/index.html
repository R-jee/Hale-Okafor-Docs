Blocks

Blocks overview

Blocks are the pieces inside a section. Hale & Okafor ships 37 theme blocks: most make up the object record on the product page; the rest are content pieces and footer columns.

#Product page blocks

The object record: the blocks inside the product information section and the alternate product templates.

BlockWhat it does
Buy buttons
buy-buttons		Quantity (respecting B2B quantity rules), subscription / selling plan choice, gift card recipient form, add to cart, express checkout buttons and pickup availability.
Certificate
certificate		A button that opens the object's certificate (studio, object name and number, measurements, materials, technique, maker, year, edition, the studio's seal and a signature line), ready to print or save as a PDF.
Collapsible row
collapsible-tab		A collapsible row (details/summary) for per-product content such as materials, care, size guide or shipping.
Complementary products
complementary-products		Complementary products ("Pairs well with") from Shopify's Recommendations API, configured in the Search & Discovery app.
Description
description		The product description, shown inline or in a collapsible row.
Edition and status
edition-state		A Sold stamp, "1 of 1", "Edition of 12, 4 remain" or "Made to order, ships in 6 weeks".
Lot bid
lot-bid		The sale room's bid sheet for an antique lot: the lot number and period, estimate, current bid and number of bids, the closing clock, and a sealed bid sent to the studio through Shopify's contact form (so it works without JavaScript).
Make an offer
make-offer		Propose a price for a piece, sent to the studio through the contact form (amount, name, email, note).
Maker
maker-card		The maker of this object, from the atelier.maker metaobject: portrait, name, where they work and a short biography.
Making log
making-log		"3 Mar · Thrown", "19 Mar · Glaze firing, 1280 °C".
Object header
object-header		Number, name as the page heading, then the wall label line (material · year · maker) and the label note.
Price
price		The selected variant's price (sale, unit price and subscription prices), the tax and shipping note, and the Shop Pay Installments banner when it's enabled for the store.
Reserve
reserve-piece		Ask the studio to hold a one-of-a-kind piece for a while (48 hours by default).
Scale drawing
scale-drawing		The object drawn to scale beside something everyone knows the size of (a coin, a hand, an A4 sheet, a chair or a person), so customers can judge its size before it arrives.
Share
share		Opens the device's native share sheet, or copies the link where that isn't available and announces "Link copied".
Specification
specification		A ledger of the object's record.
Stock
stock-signal		The selected variant's availability as a lamp and a few words: green "In stock" (with how many are left when inventory is tracked), amber "Only 3 left" at or under the threshold, "Made to order" or "Out of stock".
True size
true-scale		Shows the object at its real height on the visitor's screen.
Variant picker
variant-picker		Variant options as the Studio picker, buttons, swatches (Shopify's native swatch data) or dropdowns.

#Content blocks

Text, images, buttons, groups and spacers for sections that accept theme blocks.

BlockWhat it does
Button
button		Renders a call-to-action link styled as a button.
Custom Liquid
custom-liquid		Renders merchant-provided Liquid or HTML (app snippets, embeds, custom badges).
Delivery estimate
delivery-estimate		Estimated delivery dates for the selected variant, from the fulfillment timings in Theme settings → Shipping and delivery (shared with the cart).
Divider
divider		A horizontal rule in the color scheme's border color.
Group
group		Stack them vertically or place them side by side, with spacing, alignment and an optional color scheme and padding.
Heading
heading		Renders a heading.
Image
image		An image with optional link, shape and width.
Info drawer
info-drawer		A link that opens a side drawer with per-product information shared across products: size guide, ingredients, specifications, care.
Product rating
rating		Star rating from Shopify's standard product review metafields (reviews.rating and reviews.rating_count), which most reviews apps write.
Spacer
spacer		Vertical space between blocks, from the theme's spacing scale.
Text
text		Renders merchant rich text with a choice of body styles.
Volume pricing
volume-pricing		B2B quantity rules (minimum, maximum, increment) and volume price breaks for the selected variant.

#Footer blocks

Blocks only the footer uses (their names start with an underscore).

BlockWhat it does
Contact
_footer-contact		Contact details in the footer, each a real link where it can be: email (mailto:), phone (tel:), WhatsApp (wa.me), plus an address and opening hours.
Menu
_footer-menu		A footer column with a heading and the links of one menu, optionally followed by the store's policies (privacy, shipping, terms, refunds, contact), so the utility bar keeps to the copyright.
Newsletter
_footer-newsletter		Newsletter sign-up in the footer (snippets/newsletter-form.liquid): heading, text, an optional incentive line, the field in one of three styles, and consent text that links the privacy policy.
Social media
_footer-social		Social profiles from Theme settings → Social media, as icons, text links, or icons with labels.
Store information
_footer-store		Store or boutique information from the store location metaobject (the same entries as the store locator): name, address, opening hours, a phone link and directions.
Text
_footer-text		A footer column with a heading and rich text (the store's story), and optionally the studio's contact details under it: email (mailto:), phone (tel:) and address, each with its icon.
