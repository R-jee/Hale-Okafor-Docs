Blocks

Lot bid

The sale room's bid sheet for an antique lot: the lot number and period, estimate, current bid and number of bids, the closing clock, and a sealed bid sent to the studio through Shopify's contact form (so it works without JavaScript).

How it works

The studio confirms bids by email and keeps the current bid and bid count up to date in the lot's record (atelier.current_bid, atelier.bid_count). Once the lot closes the sheet shows the result instead of the form. Renders nothing for products that are not lots.

File
blocks/lot-bid.liquid

Used on product.lot

#Settings

#1General

Takes sealed bids for a sale-room lot through the contact form. Set the closing time, estimate, opening bid and increment in the lot's record, and update the current bid and number of bids as you confirm them.

  • ProductProductproduct
  • TermsRich textterms

    Default: Bids are sealed and confirmed by email. The highest bid when the lot closes is offered the piece.
